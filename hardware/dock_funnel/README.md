# Dock Funnel Guide-Rail — Redesign v1

Parametric replacement for the original funnel that **wedged** the robot (heavy hand-push
couldn't free it). Generated by [`funnel_rail_gen.py`](funnel_rail_gen.py).

## Why the old one jammed
- Flare widened too fast (**~30°/side**) and met the throat at a **sharp corner** → a skewed
  280 mm tongue drove its corner into the wall and **geometrically locked**.
- Throat width (280) = tongue width (280) → **zero clearance** for even 1° of skew.

## What changed in this redesign
| Change | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flare half-angle | ~30° | **22.5°** | guides a skewed tongue IN instead of catching it |
| Flare→throat corner | sharp | **R15 fillet** | kills the corner-catch — the actual lock point |
| Throat entrance | 280 | **298** | accepts ~5–6° of arrival skew |
| Throat seat | 280 | **285**, tapered from 298 | 5 mm clearance; the taper squares the tongue as it seats |
| Material | PLA | **PETG** (recommend) | slicker + tougher wear surface |

## Files
- `funnel_rail_right.stl`, `funnel_rail_left.stl` — the two side rails, ready to slice (no mounting holes — see below).
- `funnel_rail_profile.dxf` — 2D footprint of the right rail → **import into Fusion 360 as a sketch, extrude to your wall height, add your mounting holes**.
- `funnel_rail_gen.py` — edit the parameter block at the top and re-run `python3 funnel_rail_gen.py` to regenerate everything.

## ⚠️ Three things ONLY YOU can set — verify before printing
1. **`WALL_H` (rail height, default 50 mm)** — must be tall enough to catch the robot's rear tongue,
   but **short enough that the 385 mm-wide drive wheels clear it**. Measure the tongue contact height.
2. **Mounting holes** — deliberately NOT in the STL (I don't know your extrusion bolt pattern).
   Add them in Fusion (import the DXF or STL) or drill after printing to match your 20×20 frame.
3. **`total funnel depth` (FLARE_LEN + THROAT_LEN = 165 mm)** — **must stay < ~175 mm** so the drive
   wheels never enter the funnel and hit the flare. This assumes tongue length ~190 mm — confirm yours.

## Parameters (in `funnel_rail_gen.py`)
```
MOUTH_W       385   # <= drive-wheel width (385). DO NOT exceed.
THROAT_ENT_W  298   # throat entrance (tongue 280 + ~6° skew allowance)
THROAT_SEAT_W 285   # throat at the seat (tongue 280 + 5 mm clearance)
FLARE_LEN     105   # -> 22.5° half-angle
THROAT_LEN     60   # squaring runway
FILLET_R       15   # flare->throat blend
WALL_H         50   # <<< VERIFY vs tongue height AND wheel clearance
WALL_T          5   # wall thickness
```

## Also do (mechanical, pairs with this)
- **Chamfer / round the robot's rear corners** (small PETG skids) so a steel corner can't catch the rail.
- Optional: line the guide face with slick tape if you have PTFE (Kapton won't help much).

## Pairs with software
Keep the **gentle 0.08 m/s approach + firm terminal throat-push** in `dock_aligner` so the tongue
arrives at ≤~5° skew — inside what this funnel now accepts. Geometry + gentle approach together.

## Print
PETG, 3–4 perimeters, 30–40% infill, no support needed (walls are vertical). Print flat on the base.
